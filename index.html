Tell us what you think

CultureCompass is a website for travellers to explore the cultures of different countries through short stories and a small exploration game. It is built to cover many destinations and many cultures.

This is an early prototype. Only Hà Nội, Vietnam is available for now, so please judge the idea and the experience, not the number of countries.

About 5 minutes · anonymous · part of a student UX project (CSCU9X5). Fields marked * are required.
